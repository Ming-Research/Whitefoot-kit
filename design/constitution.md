# The Whitefoot-kit constitution

This constitution holds what the kit is for and its lasting values. The owner
sets it and changes it.

## What the kit is for

The kit is part of Whitefoot, kept in a repository of its own. It holds what
agents and the projects written in Whitefoot share: the guide agents write
Whitefoot from, and the means to build with a released compiler. Whitefoot's
constitution is the kit's as well; what follows is the kit's own part of it.

## Article 1 (the guide). Agents write Whitefoot from the guide

An agent that reads the guide writes programs the newest Whitefoot release
accepts. What an agent cannot write from the guide is fixed in the guide, in the
compiler's diagnostics or in the language; the guide never shows a way around a
gap.

Violations: a guide example the newest release rejects; a way around a gap of
Whitefoot in the guide.
